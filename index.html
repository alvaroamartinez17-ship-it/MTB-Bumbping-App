First run checklist
  1. Wait for GPS before you drop in. With no SIM there's no assistance data, so a cold fix takes one to three minutes instead of seconds. Stand still with a clear view of the sky until the GPS readout goes green.
  2. Phone flat on a table, screen up, then
  3. Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never.
  4. Low Power Mode off — it throttles the sensor callbacks.
  5. Keep Safari in front. Switching apps suspends the page.
  6. Runs need 30 m of vertical drop to be kept. Anything less is discarded as a dip rather than a trail — the log line below tells you what was thrown away and why.
